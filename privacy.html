RcloneVault Privacy Policy

RcloneVault is a personal OAuth application used exclusively by its owner to access Google Drive through the open-source rclone application.

Data Access

Rclone accesses Google Drive using permissions explicitly granted by the account owner.

Data Storage

Rclone operates locally on the owner's devices. OAuth credentials are maintained by the owner.

When using rclone crypt, file contents and filenames are encrypted locally before uploading to Google Drive.

Data Sharing

This website does not collect Google Drive data. The application does not sell user data or share it with third parties.
